Safety tips

Before you meet

  • Chat on the platform first and keep personal details, such as your address or workplace, private for now.
  • Ask for a video call or a recent photo to check that the person is real.
  • Search their name or photos online for anything that doesn't add up.

The first meetup

  • Meet in a public place and make your own way there and back.
  • Tell a trusted friend who you are meeting, where and when, and share your live location.
  • Keep your phone charged and stay sober enough to make clear decisions. Never leave a drink unattended.

Consent and protection

  • Say what you want and don't want. You can say no or change your mind at any moment.
  • Talk about protection and sexual health openly. Use condoms and get tested regularly.

Warning signs

  • Requests for money, gift cards or bank details.
  • Pressure to move off the platform quickly or to keep things secret.
  • Refusing a video call, or stories that keep changing.
  • Anyone who seems under 18. Leave and report right away.

If something goes wrong

Leave and get to a safe place. In an emergency call your local emergency number (112 in the Netherlands and the EU). Then block and report the member through the chat or by emailing mcleanpaulalton@gmail.com.
